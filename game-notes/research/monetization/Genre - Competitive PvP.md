# Genre — Competitive PvP

Shooters (RIVALS, Arsenal), fighting/battlegrounds (The Strongest Battlegrounds, Jujutsu Shenanigans), team modes (BedWars), social deduction (Murder Mystery 2), sports (Volleyball Legends) and Blade Ball-style arenas.

Related: [[Genre - Obby and Skill]] · [[Genre - Roleplay and Social]] · [[Topic - Minors and Purchases]] · [[Decision - 2x Luck Temporary vs Permanent]] · [[Marketing - Channels]] · [[Data - Public Game Snapshot]] · [[Sources]] · [[Roblox Monetization - Index]]

## Key takeaways

- **The oldest-skewing content:**
  - 29 "mild" and 5 "moderate" vs 18 "minimal" across 54 games, the only core genre where mild outnumbers minimal (P1).
  - **Inference:** an older, teen-heavy audience. This content rating is the strongest age signal in the data, but it describes content, not users.
- **Cosmetics, battle passes and power:**
  - VIP in **57%** (median R$ 395).
  - Bundles in 72% (R$ 975).
  - **Battle/season passes in 24%** (R$ 499), the second-highest after RPG/TD.
  - **Luck boosts in only 22%** (R$ 349), and 0 PvP games sell luck both ways.
  - **Verified** (P1)
- **Paid random cosmetics are common:** RIVALS "Skin Case" R$ 289, Jujutsu Shenanigans "1 Random Skin" R$ 25, The Strongest Battlegrounds "Random Emote" R$ 25 (P1). **Verified**
  - These fall under Roblox's **paid random items** rules (odds shown, `PolicyService` restrictions) [R12](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items). **Verified** rule.
- **Where power is sold, it's sold as permanent passes:** BedWars sells **19+ kits as R$ 399 passes** plus a R$ 799 Halloween 2026 Battle Pass (P1). **Verified**
  - The pay-to-win debate centers on these kits (player guides; search summary, URL not retained). **Reported, weak**
- **Whale-scale currency:** Murder Mystery 2 sells currency packs up to **R$ 22,500**. Its 87 currency products have a median R$ 1,700 (P1). **Verified**
- **Streak repair is a PvP habit:** 19% of PvP games sell streak buybacks (median **R$ 17**), e.g. Volleyball Legends "Streak Buyback" R$ 49 (P1). **Verified**

## Prices and bundles

- **Bundle ladders:** RIVALS steps R$ 59 → 95 → 289 → 649 → 849 → 865 → 975 → 1,425. **Verified** (P1)
- **Multi-open discounts on cases:** RIVALS "Skin Case" R$ 289 vs "[3]" R$ 545, about 37% off per case. **Verified** listing; arithmetic is **Inference**.
- **Off-sale passes become trade-only items:** MM2's Bioblade godly knife was a R$ 1,699 pass; the pass went off sale and the item is now only obtainable by trading [W11](https://murder-mystery-2.fandom.com/wiki/Bioblade_Gamepass). **Reported** (fan wiki)

## "Not enough currency" handling and buy-prompt placement

| Moment | What's sold | Share | Median |
|---|---|---|---|
| Out of currency (keys, tokens) | Currency packs | 50% | R$ 399 |
| Streak lost | Streak buyback | 19% | R$ 17 |
| Death mid-match | Revive / respawn | 17% | R$ 60 |
| Waiting | Skip / refresh tasks | 11% | R$ 39 |

Source: P1. **Verified**

**Inference:**
- PvP prompts belong *between* matches: the post-match screen, the case-opening screen, the lobby shop.
- Mid-match revives sold to one side are the most fairness-sensitive placement, so keep them out of ranked modes.

## Marketing channels

- **RIVALS growth:** RoWatcher describes RIVALS growing into "a nearly million-player" shooter [W10](https://rowatcher.com/news/rivals-how-roblox-s-top-shooter-built-a-nearly-million-player-empire). **Reported**. Its 2026-10-24 CCU was 278,282 (P1, **Verified**).
- **Competitive content travels:** clips, ranked seasons, tournaments. Season passes create a reason to return each season. **Inference**
- **Ads targeting by interest/genre reaches only opted-in 18+ users** ([M6](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-ui-targeting-enhancements/4333400)). That fits this genre better than kid-heavy ones. **Verified** rule; fit is **Inference**.

## What to copy / avoid

- **Copy:**
  - Cosmetics and battle passes.
  - Cosmetic bundles at stepped prices.
  - Streak repair at a low price.
- **Avoid:**
  - Selling luck or stat boosts that touch match outcomes.
  - If you must, keep power purchases permanent, capped and available free through play (the BedWars free-track model is the reference point; unverified).
  - Showing case odds anywhere other than *before* purchase.
