# Genre — Obby and Skill

Obstacle courses and skill/platform games: Tower of Hell, Speed Run 4, "Obby But You're On a Bike", troll towers, story obbies (Barry's Prison Run).

Related: [[Genre - Simulators]] · [[Genre - Competitive PvP]] · [[Topic - DevProducts vs Gamepasses]] · [[Marketing - Channels]] · [[Data - Public Game Snapshot]] · [[Sources]] · [[Roblox Monetization - Index]]

## Key takeaways

- **The cheapest genre:**
  - Typical pass R$ 129, typical product **R$ 42**.
  - Median 9 passes and 24 products per game (21 games).
  - Source: P1. **Verified**
- **The obby-specific product is the skip:**
  - **67%** of obbies sell a skip (median **R$ 39**).
  - **38%** sell something at the failure moment (revive, self-rescue, restore streak; median R$ 49).
  - **Verified** (P1)
- **Gear and abilities are usually passes:**
  - Coils, jetpacks, tool slots, admin.
  - Admin in **29%** of obbies (median R$ 399).
  - **Verified** (P1)
- **Lowest ratings of any genre:** median like ratio **78.5%** (17 games, P1). **Verified**
  - **Inference:** difficulty and trolling probably drive this more than monetization. I found no source linking the two.
- **The DevForum's main warning:** selling *the end of the game* (big skip bundles) undermines the challenge. Suggested instead:
  - a cheap starter bundle (about R$ 25)
  - one free skip
  - a skip reminder after repeated deaths
  - Source: [D4](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-eye-obby-monetization-offer-ui/3101784) (2024-08-04). **Reported** (community advice)
- **Low revenue per player:** community estimates put obby revenue per DAU near **$0.01**, the bottom of the genres listed ([C3](https://bloxg.com/statistics/roblox-game-revenue-statistics), unaudited). **Reported**

**Directly relevant to the luck decision:**
- **Tower of Hell sells a *timed* membership as a repeatable product** (week R$ 35 / month R$ 120) instead of a permanent pass.
- **Barry's Prison Run sells each item both as a permanent pass and as a product**, plus a R$ 20 rental.
- **Inference:** "temporary vs permanent" is a known lever outside luck games too. What the product versions grant isn't stated in the listings.

## Prices and bundles

- Most items cost **R$ 5–150**. Big bundles are rare and draw criticism: the ~R$ 5,000 offer in [D4](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-eye-obby-monetization-offer-ui/3101784) was called too much and bad for the game. **Reported**
- **DevForum obby threads (search summary, 2026-10-24) report:**
  - skip-stage products as "almost everything I earn" for some creators;
  - "skip to end" rarely bought for others;
  - a R$ 15 skip that "was not going well".
  - Sources: [D5](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/500-1000-ccu-making-1000-robux-day-help-monetization/4165417), [D6](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ideas-to-better-monetize-my-obby/1654630), [D7](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-can-i-improve-the-monetization-on-this-obby/1356923). **Reported** (anecdotes; individual posts not opened)

## "Not enough currency" handling and buy-prompt placement

| Moment | What's sold | Share | Median |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Failure** (death, fall, streak lost) | Revive / self-rescue / restore killstreak | **38%** | R$ 49 |
| Waiting (timer) | Skip timer | 24% | R$ 117 |
| Streak repair | Restore streak | 14% | R$ 29 |
| Out of coins | Coin packs | 33% | R$ 100 |

Source: P1. **Verified**

- **Community-suggested placement:** offer the skip *after repeated deaths* on the same stage, and give one free skip first so players learn the feature [D4](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-my-eye-obby-monetization-offer-ui/3101784). **Reported**
- **Roblox guideline:** don't pressure minors; use "View Item" / "Open Shop" wording [R3](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization). **Verified**

## Marketing channels

- Short, funny fail clips and troll moments suit TikTok/YouTube Shorts; troll-tower names lean into this (P1 names). **Inference**
- Speed-run leaderboards give content creators a goal. **Inference**
- **Update cadence:** "new stages" tags in game names, e.g. "[NEW STAGES] DONT SPLASH" (P1). **Verified** naming; the effect is **Inference**.

## What to copy / avoid

- **Copy:**
  - A cheap skip product (R$ 15–49), offered after repeated failure.
  - One free skip.
  - Cosmetic trails/effects as passes.
- **Avoid:**
  - Bundles that sell the ending.
  - Hard pressure wording.
  - Selling the ability to ruin other players' runs without opt-in modes.
