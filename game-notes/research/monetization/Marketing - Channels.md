# Marketing — Channels that work on Roblox

Related: [[Roblox Monetization - Index]] · [[Topic - Minors and Purchases]] · [[Topic - DevProducts vs Gamepasses]] · [[Genre - RNG and Gacha]] · [[Sources]]

## Key takeaways

- **Roblox's recommendation algorithm is the main channel.** It ranks games on:
  - play-through rate, first-play bounce, play days, playtime, co-play days, qualified sessions;
  - **spend days per user** and **Robux spent per user**.
  - Signals count **only users who arrived organically from "Recommended for You"**.
  - Ads, search, friends and social traffic don't feed the ranking directly.
  - [M1](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery). **Verified**
- **Under-16s can only play games that qualify for the Roblox Kids/Select catalog.**
  - Requirements: an ID-verified creator, a Minimal/Mild/Moderate rating, and a Plus subscription or a refundable fee.
  - Plus an engagement threshold: **250 unique plays from highly engaged players in 60 days** (docs) vs **500 highly engaged players** (July 2026 announcement). **Contradictory; check live.**
  - Expedited review: **50,000 Robux** (docs) vs **100,000 Robux** (announcement), refundable.
  - [M2](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select), [M3](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-global-launch-upcoming-updates-to-eligibility-ads-manager-and-expedited-review/4685717). **Verified** (both sources say what they say)
- **Roblox ads are cheap per play but noisy.**
  - Roblox claims cost-per-play fell "over 71%" to **under $0.01 per join**.
  - The typical advertiser saw **+24% plays, +16% playtime**.
  - "The five newest experiences to reach the top 20 by earnings all started with Sponsored Experience ads" (Sep 2025).
  - Developers report very uneven results.
  - [M4](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-for-creators-rdc-recap-and-what%E2%80%99s-next/3929106) **Verified** (Roblox's claim) / [M5](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/optimizing-my-games-cost-per-play-from-ads/3047197) **Reported** (developer anecdotes)
- **Ad targeting by interest or gender is adults-only.** Audience, genre or gender targeting shows ads **only to 18+ users who opted in** to personalized ads. For all-ages reach, use goal + device/location only. [M6](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/ads-manager-ui-targeting-enhancements/4333400). **Verified**
- **Viral growth in 2025 came from short-form video and live events.**
  - Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot spread on TikTok and YouTube.
  - Scheduled "admin abuse" events and update drops drove CCU records (Steal a Brainrot 25.4M; platform record **47.4M** during an "admin war", 2025-08-23).
  - [M7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot), [M8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden). **Reported** (press via Wikipedia)

## Application plan (for a rolling RNG game)

1. **Get into the Kids & Select catalog first**, if the audience skews under 16:
   - ID-verify, enable 2FA, rate the game Minimal/Mild/Moderate.
   - Plus subscription or fee.
   - Track *Audience Reach*.
   - The *Engagement* ad goal or expedited review can speed this up.
2. **Optimize the organic RFY signals:**
   - Low first-play bounce (fast first roll).
   - Play days (daily streak, timed events).
   - Co-play (server luck events everyone joins).
   - Spend *days* (cheap repeatable products, see [[Decision - 2x Luck Temporary vs Permanent]]).
   - **Don't** put "free Robux / reward" style text in metadata.
3. **Weekly event rhythm:**
   - Schedule a server-wide luck event as an *Experience Event* with RSVP.
   - Pair it with a notification to opted-in 13+ users.
   - Ship clips of the best pulls from that weekend.
4. **Ads:**
   - Small *Plays* campaigns to test 3–5 thumbnails.
   - Then a *Lapsed* campaign after each big update.
   - Read CPP and ROAS in Ads Manager.
   - Expect noise for the first ~24 h (learning period).
5. **Creators:** brief them on disclosure ("#ad"). No "ask your parents to buy" content (an EU-flagged practice).
6. **Risks:**
   - Ads targeting kids are contextual only.
   - Engagement-goal campaigns don't auto-pause at the catalog threshold.
   - Vendor case-study numbers are unverified.
