# Genre — Survival and Horror (added from the data)

Co-op survival and horror runs: 99 Nights in the Forest, DOORS, Dandy's World, Evade, Violence District. I added this genre because **51 of 339 charting games** fall here, more than Tycoons, RNG or Obbies.

Related: [[Genre - Competitive PvP]] · [[Genre - Obby and Skill]] · [[Decision - 2x Luck Temporary vs Permanent]] · [[Data - Public Game Snapshot]] · [[Sources]] · [[Roblox Monetization - Index]]

## Key takeaways

- **The lightest genre on passes:** median **4 passes** and 22 products per game. Typical pass R$ 199, typical product R$ 129 (P1). **Verified**
  - 99 Nights in the Forest (321k CCU at collection) has **one** pass ("Decorator Pass" R$ 199). **Verified**
- **The failure moment is the main purchase moment:**
  - **41%** sell revives (median **R$ 49**), the highest of any genre.
  - Several raise the price for each revive in the same run:
    - DOORS: "1 REVIVE" R$ 40 → "AGAIN" R$ 60 → "YET AGAIN" R$ 120.
    - 99 Nights: "Self Revive" R$ 55 → "Self Revive Again" R$ 95.
  - Source: P1. **Verified**
- **Co-op revives are sold too:** 99 Nights sells "Revive Friend" R$ 95 and "Revive Everyone" R$ 230. **Verified**
  - **Inference:** social generosity is a purchase motive.
- **Timed XP boosts in ladders:**
  - Violence District: 30% EXP boost for 1 h / 3 h / 12 h at R$ 49 / 149 / 549.
  - Evade: XP booster 15 min R$ 19 / 1 h R$ 49.
  - Source: P1. **Verified**
  - Per-hour prices barely fall with length (49 → 49.7 → 45.8 per hour in Violence District). **Inference** arithmetic.
- **Mature-leaning content:** 16 minimal / 27 mild / 8 moderate (P1). **Verified**
  - **Inference:** a teen-heavy audience, similar to PvP.

## "Not enough currency" handling and buy-prompt placement

| Moment | Share | Median |
|---|---|---|
| **Failure (death)** | **41%** | R$ 49 |
| Out of currency | 43% | R$ 100 |
| Waiting | 10% | R$ 19 |
| Streak repair | 6% | R$ 50 |

Source: P1. **Verified**

**Inference:**
- The revive offer appears on the death screen. Escalating prices limit how far a run can be bought.
- For young players, Roblox's guidance on neutral wording applies to death-screen offers too [R3](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization).

## Complaints and their causes

## Relevance to the luck decision

Survival games already sell **power as temporary or run-limited products** ("lasts 3 runs", "single life"), not permanent passes. This is the clearest example outside RNG of temporary power sold as products. **Verified** listings; the reading is **Inference**.
