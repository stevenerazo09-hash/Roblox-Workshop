# Genre — Anime RPG and Tower Defense (added from the data)

Grinding RPGs (Blox Fruits), anime unit-gacha tower defense (Anime Adventures, Vanguards, Toilet Tower Defense), and Tower Defense Simulator. These overlap heavily with [[Genre - RNG and Gacha]] because units are summoned randomly.

Related: [[Genre - RNG and Gacha]] · [[Genre - Competitive PvP]] · [[Decision - 2x Luck Temporary vs Permanent]] · [[Topic - Minors and Purchases]] · [[Data - Public Game Snapshot]] · [[Sources]] · [[Roblox Monetization - Index]]

## Key takeaways

- **The highest typical prices:**
  - Typical pass **R$ 300**, typical product **R$ 291**.
  - Bundles median **R$ 1,999**.
  - Battle/season passes in **35%** of games (median **R$ 799**), the highest share of any genre.
  - Source: P1 (20 games). **Verified**
- **The only genre with paid-entry games on the charts:** Bloodlines (R$ 799) and Rogue Lineage (R$ 200) (P1). **Verified**
- **Lots of rewarded ads and gifting:** 30% sell rewarded-ad placeholder products, and 60% sell gift products, the highest of any genre (P1). **Verified**
- **Luck is sold mostly as products:**
  - 6 games sell repeatable luck; only 1 sells a permanent luck pass.
  - Anime Adventures: "Super Lucky" R$ 99, "Ultra Lucky" R$ 149 as products.
  - Toilet Tower Defense: a permanent "Lucky" pass at R$ 149.
  - Source: P1. **Verified**
- **A clear permanent vs timed pair in Blox Fruits:**
  - "2x Mastery" pass R$ 450.
  - "EXP Boost (15 Mins)" product R$ 25, and a 24 h EXP boost R$ 1,499.
  - The pass costs about 7 hours of the cheapest-per-hour timed boost (`breakeven.csv`).
  - Source: P1. **Verified** listings; break-even is **Inference** arithmetic.

## Complaints and their causes

- **Gacha odds and reroll spending** are the obvious risks. Trait rerolls, spins and summons are paid random items under Roblox policy [R12](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items). **Verified** rule.
- **No game-specific complaint source found** in my searches.

## Relevance to the luck decision

- Unit-TD games sell **rarity luck as a permanent pass at a high price** ("Shiny Hunter" R$ 1,299). They keep **general luck mostly as cheap products**.
- **Inference:** this pattern supports the hybrid: an expensive, narrow permanent pass, plus cheap repeatable boosts.
