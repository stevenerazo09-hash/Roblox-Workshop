# Genre — Tycoons and Idle

Build-a-base and idle-income games, including the 2025–26 **"steal"** wave (Steal a Brainrot, Steal An Egg) and farming idlers (Grow a Garden), plus classic tycoons (Theme Park / Retail / Lumber Tycoon 2).

Related: [[Genre - Simulators]] · [[Genre - RNG and Gacha]] · [[Decision - 2x Luck Temporary vs Permanent]] · [[Topic - Minors and Purchases]] · [[Marketing - Channels]] · [[Data - Public Game Snapshot]] · [[Sources]] · [[Roblox Monetization - Index]]

## Key takeaways

- **Few passes, many products.**
  - Median **3 passes** vs **101 products** per game (19 games). Typical pass R$ 234, typical product R$ 199.
  - The top "steal" games sell almost everything as products: Steal a Brainrot has 3 passes and 80 products; Grow a Garden has 413 products, one per seed or egg.
  - Source: P1. **Verified**
- **The core passes are 2x income and luck.**
  - 2x money in **63%** of games (median R$ 199).
  - Luck items in **58%** (R$ 199).
  - Server-wide boosts in 26% (R$ 99).
  - **Verified** (P1)
- **Currency top-ups are near-universal:** **84%** of tycoons sell currency packs (median R$ 249), the highest of any genre. **Verified** (P1)
- **Admin passes are sold at extreme prices.**
  - 16% of tycoons sell admin powers, at a median **R$ 7,999** (Steal a Brainrot "Admin Commands" R$ 7,999). **Verified** (P1)
  - Press coverage singled out buyable "server administrator" abilities as pay-to-win (Polygon, via [M7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)). **Reported**
- **Returning players are a purchase moment:**
  - Offline-earnings boosts and claims, e.g. "Triple Offline Cash" R$ 49, "Double Offline Reward" R$ 49.
  - Timers per item, e.g. Steal a Brainrot sells a separate "Skip Timer" product for each egg, R$ 19–49.
  - **Verified** (P1)

## Prices and bundles

- **Multi-packs are priced at exact multiples.** No volume discount: Steal a Brainrot "+3 Secret Lucky Block" R$ 4,497 = 3 × 1,499, and "+8" R$ 11,992 = 8 × 1,499. **Verified** (P1)
  - **Inference:** this keeps value perception honest, but there's no bulk incentive.
- **Prices of individual blocks move over time.** A guide says the Brainrot God block dropped from R$ 599 to R$ 399; the cause isn't confirmed [W7](https://www.sportskeeda.com/roblox-news/steal-brainrot-lucky-blocks-all-lucky-blocks-price-list). Today's listing shows R$ 399 (P1). **Reported** change; current price **Verified**.
- **Contradiction:** Sportskeeda describes server luck "from 1x up to 5x for over 2,200 Robux". The current listing shows **2x / 4x / 8x at R$ 249 / 999 / 2,999** (P1). The listing wins: prices and tiers change between updates.
- **Revenue estimates vary widely:**
  - Steal a Brainrot: $1.4M–4.4M per month (Grey Journal, model estimate [W8](https://greyjournal.net/hustle/how-much-does-steal-a-brainrot-make/)).
  - Grow a Garden: about $12M in May 2025 (CreatorExchange estimate cited by press). Other model estimates are up to about 20× lower.
  - **Reported** (estimates only; no primary disclosure).

## "Not enough currency" handling and buy-prompt placement

| Moment | What's sold | Share of tycoons | Median price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Out of currency** | Cash packs | **84%** | R$ 249 |
| **Shortfall** (can't afford the next upgrade) | Instant buy / unlock floor / skip tier | 32% | R$ 39 |
| **Waiting** (egg timer, growth, restock) | Skip timer / restock | 32% | R$ 49 |
| **Streak loss** | Missed-day / streak buyback | 16% | R$ 29 |
| **Return after being away** | Offline cash multipliers | several games | R$ 49 |

Source: P1 (`moments-by-genre.csv`). **Verified**

**Placement observed** (P2, 2026-10-22):
- **Steal An Egg:**
  - Currency bottom-left with a **"+"** button.
  - An **in-world upgrade billboard** ("Upgrade Pen, Level 1 > Level 2, $1M").
  - **"OFFLINE CLAIM! $6M"** text over the base.
  - An event countdown.
- **Steal a Brainrot:** a 2×2 left menu with Shop; shop cards with odds %, countdowns and a "Gift Player" button.
- **Grow a Garden:** a "What's New" popup on join, a "+" by the premium currency, and an "Update In …" countdown.

**Inference:** tycoons attach a product to *the exact thing the player is looking at*: this egg's timer, this floor, this seed. That's why product counts reach the hundreds.

## Marketing channels

- **Big live events:**
  - Admin-abuse events temporarily boost luck for the whole server (Wikipedia, [M7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)).
  - Brand concerts, e.g. Bruno Mars in Steal a Brainrot; see [[Marketing - Channels]].
  - **Reported**
- **Weekly drops with "be online to claim" items:** Grow a Garden's exclusive weekly items push retention ([M8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)). **Reported**
- **Paid ads aren't a sure thing for small tycoons:** one DevForum poster spent about 8k Robux on ads for a castle tycoon and made about 1k Robux back [D8](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/help-with-monetization/1372497). **Reported** (one anecdote, via search summary)
- **Copycat naming:** "Steal An Egg", "Break and Steal an Egg", "Fake an Egg", "Steal an Clown Egg" all chart together (P1).
  - **Inference:** players search by the trend word, so trend timing matters more than ads in this genre.

## What to copy / avoid

- **Copy:**
  - Products tied to *this* item or timer.
  - An honest offline-earnings boost.
  - A server luck x2/x4/x8 ladder.
  - Cheap per-item entry prices.
- **Avoid:**
  - Admin-power sales (reputation and press risk).
  - Paid random blocks without displayed odds and a policy check ([R12](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)).
  - Fake scarcity, such as a restock timer that resets for the same item ([R3](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization)).
